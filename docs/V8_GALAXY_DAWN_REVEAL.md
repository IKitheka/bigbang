# V8: dark ages into first galaxies — cinematic reveal

This is a visual-development continuation of the user's native C++/OpenGL
Big Bang animation. The existing V7 0–50-second expansion is left unchanged,
as are the QGP, nucleosynthesis and recombination chapters. The last 40 seconds
of solar-system formation and Earth reveal remain the owner-approved baseline.

## Visual diagnosis from recovered Work V4 film

At presentation time ~176–195s the neutral dark ages are deliberately
almost black. Around ~200–225s the old milky haze overwhelms most of the
image, hiding the established 4,400 3D catalogue galaxies. The haze abruptly
clears by ~240s to expose a very rich field of stars and galaxies.

## V8 production shader changes

- In cosmic_late.glsl replace the broad grey fog with a restrained, spatially
  correlated blue-violet mist, ramped up over legacy time 238–262 and down
  263–285, then strictly zero (no affected shots after ~244 film seconds).
- Increase the visibility of the original spatially registered compact
  first-light knots, *not* thousands of untracked screen-aligned sparkles.
- Reveal the same world-space 4,400 galaxies on a slightly smoother early curve
  while preserving full intensity and all later shots by legacy time 273.
- Keep the neutral dark ages astrophysically dark; faint large-scale structure
  is an illustrative density cue, not actual stars before cosmic dawn.
- Do **not** modify the native solar-system renderer, exposure/bloom,
  previous expansion effect, or five-minute total narrative timeline.

## Preview and validation

The native 40s film-time 190–230 second clip is generated by the actual
C++/GLSL/HDR engine, with the original synthesized stereo score. No reused
movie frames are injected into the native render. A 480x270/30 FPS diagnostic
preview is used to limit CI rendering costs. High-resolution production
quality must be verified before final assembly.

The acceptance gate is:
- Full C++ build and OpenGL shader compilation passes
- 1,200 consecutive native frames at 30 FPS, 40 seconds
- H.264 yuv420p video with AAC stereo soundtrack and full decode
- Dark-ages frames remain appropriately dark, transition has no grey whiteout
- Original V7 frames for 245s onward / solar finale match source protections
- Owner reviews before any 9,000-frame rerender, branch merge or release
